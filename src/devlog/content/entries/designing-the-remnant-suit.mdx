## Armed or offline

The suit has two states, and the player chooses between them. Offline, traversal is just traversal: no Remnants. Armed, qualifying movement actions can generate them.

[Which movement actions qualify, and why those.]

## The rules

- The suit sustains up to three active Remnants.
- Each Remnant lasts five seconds unless it is triggered earlier.
- Detonation or expiration releases its sustain slot immediately.
- Full capacity never disables the movement ability underneath.

<DesignNote kind="constraint" title="Full capacity never blocks movement">
  The suit adds to movement; it never takes movement away. With three Remnants
  active, the player can still perform every movement action. They just don't
  generate a fourth Remnant until a slot is free.
</DesignNote>
