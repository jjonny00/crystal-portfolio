Second Sentence is a movement-driven third-person action shooter set inside an enormous interstellar prison cell.

The prison offers lethal contracts to reduce the player's sentence. It never lets anyone out. Instead, it warps bounded sections of distant worlds into the cell, and the contract plays out there. The player never leaves.

## Contracts

Every contract is a trade: risk for time. Finish it, and the sentence gets shorter. Zero Day is the day the sentence reaches zero.

## Echo Suits

Echo Suits are equipment. Each one changes how movement and combat interact, so the suit a player wears changes how they read a space. The Remnant Suit is the first.
